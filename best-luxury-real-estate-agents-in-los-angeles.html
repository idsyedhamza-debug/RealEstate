What to look for in a Los Angeles luxury agent

  • Local depth. Neighborhood-level comps and school knowledge beat city-wide averages.
  • Negotiation credentials. Certified negotiation training protects your position beyond standard offer-and-counter.
  • Marketing reach. Professional staging, photography, and targeted campaigns matter in the high-end segment.
  • Communication cadence. Weekly progress reports and a single point of contact keep stressful moves calm.
  • Investment literacy. Even owner-occupants benefit from an agent who understands cash flow and development potential.

Final word

Each agent on this list earns a spot through verified results, transparent communication, and credentials that go beyond a license. Start with Andrew Liberty if you want commercial-grade negotiation applied to a residential move, and Stefanie Pollack if you value deep San Fernando Valley roots and a neighborhood-first approach. If you need ultra-luxury representation or Westside estates, Ryan Jancula, Danny Brown, and David Kelmenson are equally strong options. Reach out to any of them - a short consultation costs nothing and usually clarifies the right fit immediately.
